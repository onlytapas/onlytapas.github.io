Selected work

Things I have built.

The practices, platforms and AI tools my teams and I have created, and that the practice now runs on.

AI in the lifecycle

AI at every stage, people on every call

A model for using AI across the whole testing lifecycle: understanding requirements, planning, test design, test data, execution and reporting. AI does the first pass; a tester always reviews, decides and signs off.

AI agents

QA agents that take the routine

A build agent that runs smoke and sanity checks on every new build and certifies it ready or failed. A retest agent that takes a defect ticket, verifies the fix, adds comments and screenshots, and closes or reopens it, with no manual effort.

Route to live

Clear gates, no surprises

Entry and exit criteria for every stage of a release, from design review through technical QA and UAT to production sanity checks, so everyone knows what "ready" means and a QA sign-off carries weight.

Process and practice

One standard, every team

The foundation everything else runs on: standards for test strategy and test plans, test case design, defect management and reporting, built into one testing lifecycle with shared templates, checklists and quality metrics. Whoever is on the project, the quality bar is the same.

QA Labs

Every framework, one front door

A self-service hub where every QA framework and tool the practice has built lives in one place, behind a simple front end. Any team can run them straight away, with no repositories to clone and nothing to set up, so good engineering is reused instead of rebuilt project by project.
